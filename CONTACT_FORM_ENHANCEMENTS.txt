## 🎯 How It Works

### Form Submission Flow:

1. **User fills form** → Enters all required information
2. **Checks "I am human"** → Required to submit
3. **JavaScript captures timezone** → Automatic, hidden field
4. **Form submits** → AJAX POST to contact-handler.php
5. **PHP validates** → Checks all fields including human verification
6. **PHP collects tracking** → IP, browser, OS, timezone, etc.
7. **Email sent** → Beautiful HTML email with all data
8. **Auto-reply sent** → Customer receives confirmation
9. **Logged** → Entry saved to inquiries.log with tracking data
