FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

Our minimum order quantity varies by product category. For agricultural products, we typically require a minimum of 1 ton per order. For flowers, the minimum is usually 500 stems. However, we're flexible and can discuss custom requirements based on your specific needs. Contact us for detailed MOQ information for specific products.

We offer various packing options to suit different requirements: corrugated boxes, wooden crates, vacuum-sealed bags, refrigerated containers, and custom branded packaging. All packaging meets international export standards and ensures product freshness during transit. We can also accommodate specific packaging requests based on destination country regulations.

Delivery time depends on the destination and shipping method. For air freight, most destinations receive shipments within 3-7 days. Sea freight typically takes 15-30 days depending on the port. We provide real-time tracking information and work with reliable logistics partners to ensure timely delivery. Express shipping options are also available for urgent orders.

We hold multiple certifications including ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management, HACCP certification for food safety, organic certifications (USDA Organic, EU Organic), and phytosanitary certificates. We also comply with GLOBALG.A.P. standards and can provide all necessary export documentation including certificates of origin, quality certificates, and health certificates as required by importing countries.

We stand behind the quality of our products. If you receive damaged or substandard products, please notify us within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. We will arrange for replacement or credit as appropriate. Our quality guarantee covers manufacturing defects and damage during transit. We work with insurance providers to ensure your shipments are protected.
